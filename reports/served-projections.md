**Variance or model?** One gameweek cannot answer that, and this report does not pretend to. A player projected at 6 who blanked is the ordinary shape of football; the same player blanking for six weeks is a model finding. **Guardrail 10: do not re-fit on one gameweek.** What this table is for is accumulating — the misses that repeat are the ones worth chasing, and they are only visible once several of these exist.

**And grade the decision, not the scoreboard** (guide §6). A −4 hit worth +7 expected points that returned −2 was a good decision badly rewarded. The columns above measure the prediction; whether the recommendation was right is a separate question and is not answered by a bad week.
