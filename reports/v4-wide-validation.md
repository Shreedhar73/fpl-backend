# v4 on ten seasons — validation only

Does a gradient-boosted model get better with more seasons where the decomposed model does not? Measured at the only place a question may still be asked of this archive: VALIDATE, 2024-25 from round 20. No TEST row is read, no model is emitted, nothing under `src/modules/projections/v4` is touched. Generated by `tools/fit-v4/wide-validation.py`.

## The reading

| position | three seasons | ten seasons | Δ | relative |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GKP | 1.6073 | 1.5887 | **−0.0186** | −1.16% |
| DEF | 1.7928 | 1.7886 | −0.0042 | −0.23% |
| MID | 1.9145 | 1.9106 | −0.0039 | −0.20% |
| FWD | 2.0413 | 2.0449 | +0.0036 | +0.18% |

**The gradient-boosted model does get better on more seasons, and the decomposed one does not.** That
asymmetry is the finding: three of four positions improve, keepers by a full percent, on 5× the
training rows — while the same widening cost the decomposed model 0.6% of points-captured@11 and its
own per-fold selection chose two seasons every time it had a real choice.

**And it is far too small to move anything.** A 0.2% RMSE improvement on a half-season of validation
rounds is not a decision, on three counts: RMSE is not the metric this project decides on (D-020 —
the optimiser asks which eleven, not what a player scores); no standard error is attached to any of
these numbers; and forwards, the position where a haul actually decides a gameweek, got worse. The
only reading with any weight is the keepers', and keepers are the position whose minutes are most
nearly deterministic, so more history helping there is the least surprising place for it to help.

**What was NOT done, deliberately.** No model was emitted, no TEST row was read, and nothing under
`src/modules/projections/v4` was touched. The archive holdout is spent (D-034), so a v4 candidate
that wanted adopting would have to be measured on the rolling-origin referee or prospectively on the
live season — and neither is worth spending on an effect this size.
