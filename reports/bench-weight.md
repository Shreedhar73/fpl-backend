Generated by `pnpm optimize:bench-sweep`. Each weight re-picks the opening fifteen under that weight and walks the whole archived season under the real rules, through the same simulator `pnpm decision-quality` uses.

**1.0 is the behaviour B-023 replaced** — every bench place worth exactly a starting place, which is a quantity FPL never pays out.

Best under `greedy-1ft`: **0.2** at 1893 points. Spread across the weights that completed a season: **26 points** over 38 gameweeks.

**Read the spread before the winner.** One simulated season is one draw, and a difference of a few points over 38 gameweeks is not a measurement — it is the same noise that made D-023 a rule. If the column above is flat, the honest statement is that the season simulator cannot tell these weights apart, and the shipped value is a POLICY constant carrying the structural argument (a bench place is worth less than a starting place) rather than a fitted one.

What the sweep CAN establish is the direction: whether the old behaviour, weight 1.0, is worse than a discounted bench at all. That is the claim B-023 makes, and it is the one to read off the bottom row.
